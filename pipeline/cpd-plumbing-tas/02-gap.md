## Not run, and why

**No keyword research, competitor scan or GSC query analysis was performed for this page.** Stated
rather than omitted, per the repo's own rule that a deliberate skip is fine and a silent one is not.

Reasons: the page is `noindex` and blocked on a purchase path, so it cannot compete for anything
until that clears; and no per-page GSC export exists for a URL that has never been published. The
site-wide export in `business data/GSC/` cannot substitute — Queries and Pages are separate
dimensions in it and are not crossed, so it yields no per-URL query list.

**This must run before `noindex` comes off.** A CPD bundle page competes on trade-and-state intent
("plumber cpd tasmania", "cbos cpd points") and none of that has been checked against what ABE
Education already ranks for. Cannibalisation against `/cpd-tas` and `/cpd` is the specific risk:
all three target Tasmanian CPD.
