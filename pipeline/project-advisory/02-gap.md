# 02 · Demand grounding + competitor gap — `/project-advisory`

**No GSC export usable** — new URL, no history. Neil Patel connector used as the demand layer, per
Stage 2's own instruction. Brisbane (`locId 1000339`) as the AU triangulation point.

## A · The finding that should change how this page is built

**Every keyword this product could target is (a) tiny and (b) qualified with "free".** Measured, not
impressionistic:

| Seed | Volume | CPC | SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| owner builder cost calculator | 10/mo | $0 | 5 |
| building budget spreadsheet | 10/mo | $0 | 13 |
| owner builder templates | **0/mo** | $0 | 12 |
| construction budget template | 10/mo | $0 | 39 |

Nothing in the expansion set exceeds **10/mo**, and **every single term returns $0 CPC** — nobody is
bidding on any of it, in a market where "owner builder insurance" carries a $20.22 CPC.

And the expansion is saturated with the word *free*. Counted from the connector's own output:
`construction budget template free`, `residential construction budget template excel free`,
`free construction budget template excel`, `free home building cost breakdown spreadsheet`,
`construction cost tracking spreadsheet free`, `free residential construction budget template excel`,
`free house building budget template`, `free new home construction budget spreadsheet`,
`free home construction budget spreadsheet`, `free home building budget spreadsheet` — **ten explicit
"free" variants**, against zero variants signalling willingness to pay.

**Conclusion: this page must not be built as an SEO play.** The intent behind these queries is
"give me a spreadsheet for nothing", and a page selling one for $89 cannot win them and should not
try. Writing it to chase "construction budget template" would produce a page that ranks for traffic
that will never convert, and reads as a template farm to the buyer who *would* convert.

## B · SERP shape, confirming it

Top 10 for "owner builder cost calculator": two owner-builder competitor sites (DA 17, DA 18), an
insurance council calculator page, an architect's calculator, a **council PDF**, a quantity
surveyor's app, an estimating service, and — at position 10 — a page whose title literally begins
**"Free Construction Cost Calculator"**. Click counts across the whole page are 0 to 3.

Nothing here is a paid product. The SERP is free tools and lead-generation for services, which is
what the "free"-qualified queries deserve.

## C · Where this page's traffic actually comes from

**Internal, not search.** `page-type-engine.md` prescribes `/project-advisory` as a cross-link from
all five owner-builder state pages and a downlink from the `/owner-builder-courses` hub — the hub
alone carries **59.9k impressions**, the biggest single page on the site, and WA owner builder
carries 34.7k. A reader arriving from those pages has already decided to build, already paid for a
course, and is the only person for whom an $89 project toolkit is an easy yes.

So the page is a **conversion page for warm internal traffic**, and its success measure is not
ranking. That decision changes Stage 3: no keyword-stuffed H1, no competing with free-template
pages, and the opening does not need to establish what a construction budget is — the reader knows.
It needs to answer "is this worth $89 to me", fast.

## D · Competitor gap

The only genuinely comparable products are the two owner-builder competitor sites at DA 17-18, both
of which give a calculator away free as a lead magnet. **ABE Education cannot out-free them and
should not try.** What the pack has that a free calculator does not, taken straight from the
component list in `01-source-map.md` §C:

| Gap | Why it is one |
|---|---|
| The 20% contingency is **applied automatically**, not left to the user | Every free template assumes the user knows to add contingency and what rate. This is the single most common owner-builder budgeting failure |
| It tracks **progress claims and amounts owing**, not just estimates | A free budget template is a static estimate. This follows the money through the build |
| Contract terms in **plain English**, plus trade-scoping guidance | The free tools are spreadsheets only. Nobody gives away the contracts explainer |
| It is from a provider the reader has **already bought a course from** | Trust is already established at the point of the cross-link. That is worth more here than any ranking |

## Primary and secondary keywords (for Stage 3, and deliberately modest)

- **Primary:** project advisory pack — near-zero volume, and correct anyway: it is the product's
  name, it is what the cross-link anchor says, and brand-name queries are the only ones this page
  should own.
- **Secondary:** owner builder cost calculator, owner builder budget spreadsheet, owner builder
  contracts calculator — targeted for relevance and for the AI-overview surface, **not for volume**.
- **Explicitly not targeted:** anything containing "free", "template" or "download". Recorded so a
  later optimisation pass does not "improve" the page by chasing them.

## Outcome expectation, stated up front so the 4- and 12-week reviews are honest

**This page will not rank for meaningful volume and that is not a failure.** Judge it on
internal click-through from the owner-builder cluster and on conversion of that traffic, not on
impressions. A future review reading "0 clicks from search" against this file should read it as
designed.
