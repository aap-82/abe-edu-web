## Gap and how this page handles it

1. **Two competitors (whitecardwebinars.com.au, trainingaid.edu.au) actively advertise online ACT
   White Card delivery.** The regulation does not forbid it (`01-source-map.md`), so this is not a
   false claim on their part necessarily — but it means a reader landing on this page may arrive
   expecting an online option ABE's own product does not offer. **The right move, per
   `content-craft.md`'s "say the unhelpful thing": answer this directly and early**, most naturally in
   the FAQ ("Can I do this online?" → No, face-to-face, here's why that's still a real
   AlertForce-delivered card) rather than let a reader discover the mismatch after paying.
2. **AlertForce's own Canberra page ranks above this build (position 6).** Worth recording as an
   observation, not a defect this page can fix: ABE and its own RTO partner may be competing for the
   identical query. Not actionable from a build session (a commercial/channel question, not a content
   one) — flagged in the demand list rather than silently noticed and dropped.
3. **CIT (Canberra Institute of Technology) is the strongest local-authority competitor** (position 4,
   domain authority 43, a recognisable local government-linked institution) — the kind of trust
   competitor a private national RTO page needs to answer directly (why AlertForce/ABE, not the local
   TAFE-equivalent) rather than ignore. This page's RTO-verification section (the "check RTO 91826
   yourself, we would rather you did" pattern already used on WA/TAS/QLD) does that job.
4. **No competitor names the government card-fee figure ($47.00/$44.00) or the 60-day/ACT Digital
   Account application mechanic** in its ranking snippet — genuine price/process transparency this
   page can lead with, matching the same gap ABE already wins on WA/TAS.
