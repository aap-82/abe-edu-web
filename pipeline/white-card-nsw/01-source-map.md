## 4. Authority-model checks applied to this run

- ABE Education is **not** the RTO and is **not** the developer. Upskill Institute develops, delivers,
  assesses and issues. One `Person` node only (the reviewer); the RTO is credited as `Course.creator`.
- "Nationally recognised" is **accurate** here and may be used. "Statement of Attainment" is correct.
- ASQA disclosure applies in all seven locations per `kb/rules/asqa-disclosure-framework.md`.
- **The delivery claim is the sharpest risk on this page.** Never "self-paced", never "at your own
  pace", never "start any time" — S3 prohibits exactly that within connected delivery, and the 2019
  loophole this closed is the reason the rule exists. The page sells a **scheduled, trainer-led,
  real-time session**.
- Never "SafeWork NSW-approved course". Upskill holds the SafeWork NSW agreement; ABE does not.
