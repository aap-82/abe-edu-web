# 03 — Section briefs — `white-card-nsw`

Archetype **02 — nationally recognised course**. Authority model **asqa-accredited**.
Written from `01-source-map.md` (facts) and `02-gap.md` (what the market leaves open).

Section order follows the archetype's decision order: is it real → will it be accepted / can I even do
it this way → what do I get and when → price → how it works → proof → FAQ. Eight sections. This reader
comparison-shops hard and will not read a long page, so depth that does not change a decision is cut.

**Page-level H1:** `White Card NSW` — carries the primary keyword `white card nsw` (3,600 vol, SD 37)
verbatim.

**Applies to every section:** no "self-paced", no "at your own pace", no "start any time", no "instant".
SafeWork NSW prohibits self-paced learning inside connected delivery, and this page's whole credibility
rests on getting that right. Never "SafeWork NSW-approved course" — Upskill holds the agreement, ABE
does not.

## S1 · `#real` · marker 01 · "Is this a real White Card?"

**This section is required and must be authored.** `CourseLayout` renders the `PartnerDisclosure`
card from `partnerRto` frontmatter, but that card carries no H2, no capsule and no sources. Without
this section the page has nothing on its own core claim. Filed as a defect twice before, on
`white-card-tas` and `white-card-wa`.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | This is the genuine, nationally recognised construction induction credential, and the organisation standing behind it is named and checkable. |
| **Reader arrives** | Knowing they need a card, having seen a dozen near-identical offers, unable to tell which are real. |
| **Objection defused** | "How do I know this isn't a worthless certificate from a fake provider?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | Unit CPCWHS1001, *Prepare to work safely in the construction industry* (training.gov.au, 1 Aug 2026) · delivered and assessed by Upskill Institute Pty Ltd, RTO 45708, registration **Current** to 08/Jul/2030 (training.gov.au, 1 Aug 2026) · CPCWHS1001 on that RTO's scope as **Deliver and assess** since 08/Apr/2022 (training.gov.au, 1 Aug 2026) · credential is a **Statement of Attainment** · in NSW an RTO also needs an agreement with SafeWork NSW on top of ASQA registration (SafeWork NSW, 1 Aug 2026) |
| **Distinctive material** | The NSW-specific double lock. Every competitor cites an RTO number; none explains that in NSW an ASQA-registered RTO **still** cannot deliver this training without a separate SafeWork NSW agreement. That is the fact that separates a real NSW provider from a national one selling into NSW. |
| **Carrier** | `Section` + `AnswerCapsule` + short body + `ResourceLink` to training.gov.au/organisation/details/45708 + `VerifiedSources` |
| **Fails if** | The reader cannot, from this section alone, look up the RTO themselves and confirm it. |

## S2 · `#online` · marker 02 · "Can you do the NSW White Card online?"

**The page's differentiator.** Highest-value gap in `02-gap.md`; no competitor answers it straight.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | Yes, entirely online and without travelling — but as a scheduled session with a live trainer, because NSW does not allow the self-paced version sold in some other states. |
| **Reader arrives** | Having seen "$39, online, instant" offers aimed at WA and TAS, and wondering whether the NSW price is a rip-off or the cheap ones are a trap. |
| **Objection defused** | "Other sites let me start right now for less. Why does this one make me book a time?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | SafeWork NSW: "Face-to-face delivery is the preferred training delivery mode." · Connected delivery = live video conferencing providing real-time identity verification separate from training, real-time interaction and participation, and assessment by direct observation and verbal assessment · **"Connected delivery must not include any pre-training, self-paced learning; or pre-recorded trainer videos or teaching course content."** · delivery with no real-time interaction is not an accepted mode (all SafeWork NSW general conditions, 1 Aug 2026) · student must be physically in NSW during the session (Upskill, 1 Aug 2026) |
| **Distinctive material** | Turn the price objection into the trust signal. The cheaper self-paced courses are not a better deal for a NSW resident, they are the wrong product — and a card obtained that way is the exact loophole regulators closed. Say this plainly and the page has answered the one question the whole category avoids. |
| **Carrier** | `Section` + `AnswerCapsule` + `CanCant` (what NSW permits vs what it does not) + `VerifiedSources` |
| **Fails if** | It reads as an apology for being more expensive rather than an explanation of what the law requires. Also fails if it disparages WA or TAS delivery, which is entirely legitimate in those states. |
| **Do not** | Link sideways to `/white-card-wa` or `/white-card-tas` here. Competing spokes, and wrong for this reader. |

## S3 · `#accepted` · marker 03 · "Where is a NSW White Card accepted?"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | The card is nationally recognised — it travels with you across Australia, and cards from other states are accepted on NSW sites too. |
| **Reader arrives** | Possibly already holding an interstate card, possibly planning to work across a border, unsure whether they are about to pay for something they already have. |
| **Objection defused** | "I've got a QLD card / I'm moving to Victoria. Do I need to buy this at all?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | SafeWork NSW: "Construction workers can work in NSW using construction induction cards issued by other states and territories." (1 Aug 2026) · CPCWHS1001 is the same nationally recognised unit across Australia · the card becomes void after two consecutive years or more without construction work (SafeWork NSW, 1 Aug 2026) |
| **Distinctive material** | Serves `qld white card in nsw` and `wa white card in nsw`, live query clusters no competitor answers. It also tells some readers not to buy, which is the reassurance-first posture `DESIGN.md` §7 asks for and the strongest credibility move available on a page that is otherwise selling. |
| **Carrier** | `Section` + `AnswerCapsule` + short body + `Note variant="caution"` for the two-year lapse rule + `VerifiedSources` |
| **Fails if** | A reader with a valid interstate card cannot tell whether they need to enrol. |

## S4 · `#your-card` · marker 04 · "What do you get, and when does the card arrive?"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | You can work the next day on the statement you are emailed; the plastic card follows by post, and the RTO lodges the application for you. |
| **Reader arrives** | Often needing to be on site this week. The gap between finishing training and holding a card is the sharpest post-purchase anxiety in the category. |
| **Objection defused** | "If the card takes a month, am I stuck off site until it turns up?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | The RTO lodges the card application with SafeWork NSW after training (SafeWork NSW / Service NSW, 1 Aug 2026) · interim statement of training is valid **60 days** (SafeWork NSW, 1 Aug 2026) · "Your white card will usually arrive within 30 days." (Service NSW, 1 Aug 2026) · Statement of Attainment issued by Upskill Institute · once issued, the card can be accessed digitally through the Service NSW app (Service NSW, 1 Aug 2026) |
| **Distinctive material** | Nobody in the competitor set explains the 60/30 relationship — that the statement's validity deliberately outruns the card's delivery time, so there is no gap where you cannot work. That single sentence removes the fear. The digital card is uncovered by every competitor too. |
| **Carrier** | `Section` + `AnswerCapsule` + `FactGrid` (statement 60 days · card ~30 days · issued by Upskill Institute · digital card via Service NSW) + `VerifiedSources` |
| **Fails if** | The reader still cannot answer "can I work on Monday?" |

| Field | Content |

| **Reader arrives** | Price-comparing across offers whose headline figures exclude the card fee, the assessment, or both. |
| **Objection defused** | "What's the real total once the add-ons land?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | Price **$129** (internal, confirmed by Andrey 1 Aug 2026) · all-inclusive, covering training, assessment and the card (internal, confirmed 1 Aug 2026) · Service NSW confirms the training course fee is set independently by the RTO (1 Aug 2026) |

| **Guardrail** | `priceNumber` must equal `price` and both must equal `Course.offers.price`. |

## S6 · `#how-it-works` · marker 06 · "How does the session run?"

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | One booked day with a live trainer, and four things you must have ready before it starts. |
| **Reader arrives** | Ready to buy, wanting to know what the day actually demands of them. |
| **Objection defused** | "What am I actually signing up for, and can I fail it?" |
| **Facts that prove it** | Up to 7 hours of trainer-led delivery via video conference, including theory, practical assessment and breaks (Upskill, 1 Aug 2026) · student supplies their own PPE: hi-vis vest or shirt, hard hat, ear plugs, safety glasses (Upskill) · USI required · 100 points of ID · minimum age 14 (Service NSW, 1 Aug 2026) · must be physically located in NSW during the session (Upskill) · assessment in English, no external assistance, LLN assessment applies (Upskill) |
| **Distinctive material** | The PPE requirement is the one that catches people out — you supply your own hard hat and hi-vis and you put them on in front of the trainer, because the assessment includes a PPE demonstration. Competitors omit it and students arrive unable to complete the assessment. |
| **Carrier** | `Section` + `AnswerCapsule` + `Stepper` (book → prepare → attend → assessed → statement emailed) + `Note variant="caution"` for the PPE and ID requirements + `VerifiedSources` |
| **Fails if** | A reader could book a session and turn up unable to complete it. |
| **⚠ Open** | Session frequency and the booking route are **unknown** (`01-source-map.md` §3). Until Andrey answers, this section must not state or imply how soon a session is available. Write it mode-accurate and schedule-silent. |

## S8 · `#faq` · marker 08

No answer capsule (FAQ is the documented exception). Questions taken from real GSC queries and the
competitor gap, not invented:

1. Can I get a White Card online in NSW? *(direct GSC query)*
2. Why can't I do a self-paced White Card in NSW when other states can?
3. How long does the course take?
4. What do I need before the session starts?
5. I have a White Card from another state. Do I need a NSW one? *(serves `qld white card in nsw`, `wa white card in nsw`)*
6. How long until my card arrives, and can I work before then?
7. Does my White Card expire? *(serves `white card validity nsw`; the answer is the two-year lapse rule, not an expiry date)*
8. Who issues the Statement of Attainment?

**Fails if** a decision-critical fact appears only here. Price, delivery mode and the card timeline all
live in the body; the FAQ repeats, it does not carry.
