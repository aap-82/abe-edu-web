## S1 · `#real` · "Is this a real White Card?"

**Answer capsule (52 words)**

> Yes. The training is delivered and assessed by Upskill Institute, RTO 45708, and you receive a
> nationally recognised Statement of Attainment for CPCWHS1001. In New South Wales an RTO needs two
> things to run this course: registration with ASQA, and a separate agreement with SafeWork NSW.
> Upskill Institute holds both.

**Body**

Most providers show you an RTO number. That number is worth checking, and it is worth knowing what it
does not tell you on its own. Registration with ASQA lets an organisation deliver nationally
recognised training. It does not, by itself, let anyone deliver construction induction training in New
South Wales. SafeWork NSW requires a separate agreement, and its position is plain: only RTOs
accredited by ASQA with an agreement from SafeWork NSW can deliver this training in the state.

Upskill Institute Pty Ltd is RTO 45708, registered until July 2030, with CPCWHS1001 on its scope to
both deliver and assess. You can confirm all of that on training.gov.au, the national register, before
you pay us anything. We would rather you did.

Upskill Institute issues your Statement of Attainment. ABE Education publishes this course, enrols you
and supports you through it, and is not a Registered Training Organisation.

## S2 · `#online` · "Can you do the NSW White Card online?"

**Answer capsule (57 words)**

> Yes, entirely online, with no travel and no classroom. What you cannot do in New South Wales is
> complete it at your own pace. SafeWork NSW requires a live trainer in real time, so the course runs
> as a booked session on video rather than as a course you click through whenever it suits you.

**Body**

If you have been comparing prices, you have seen White Cards advertised for around forty dollars,
finished the same hour, no appointment. Those are real courses. They are also not available to you as
a New South Wales resident, and the reason is worth two minutes of your time.

Fully self-paced online White Card training is permitted in Western Australia and Tasmania. Everywhere
else, including New South Wales, the training has to happen face to face. SafeWork NSW accepts live
video delivery as face to face, which is why this course can be done from your kitchen table, but it
sets a hard limit on what that delivery can contain. In its conditions for training organisations,
SafeWork NSW states that connected delivery "must not include any pre-training, self-paced learning;
or pre-recorded trainer videos or teaching course content".

So a trainer runs the session live, verifies who you are, watches you demonstrate the practical
components and assesses you by direct observation. That is the requirement, not a service level ABE
Education invented.

**CanCant**

*In New South Wales you can:*
- Complete the whole course online from home or a work site, with no travel to a classroom
- Ask the trainer questions in real time and be assessed on the spot
- Choose a weekday, a Saturday, or the Tuesday evening class

*In New South Wales you cannot:*
- Work through the course at your own pace whenever it suits you
- Complete it by watching pre-recorded videos
- Use a self-paced course bought for another state to satisfy the NSW requirement
- Sit the session from outside New South Wales

**Verified line.** ✓ VERIFIED · SOURCES — SafeWork NSW, general conditions for registered training
organisations delivering training and assessment. Verified 1 August 2026.

## S3 · `#accepted` · "Where is a NSW White Card accepted?"

**Answer capsule (49 words)**

> On construction sites anywhere in Australia. CPCWHS1001 is a nationally recognised unit, so the card
> you earn in New South Wales travels with you interstate. It works the other way too: SafeWork NSW
> accepts construction induction cards issued by other states and territories on NSW sites.

**Body**

If you already hold a White Card from Queensland, Western Australia or anywhere else in Australia, you
probably do not need to buy this course. SafeWork NSW says construction workers can work in New South
Wales using construction induction cards issued by other states and territories. Check your card is
still valid, and if it is, keep your money.

There is one condition that catches people out, and it has nothing to do with which state issued the
card. A construction induction card becomes void if you have not carried out construction work for two
consecutive years or more. If you have been out of the industry that long, the card in your wallet is
no longer doing anything, wherever it came from, and you will need to complete the training again.

**Note (caution).** Been out of construction for two years or more? Your existing card is void,
whichever state issued it, and you will need to do the training again before you return to site.

**Verified line.** ✓ VERIFIED · SOURCES — SafeWork NSW, white cards. Verified 1 August 2026.

## S4 · `#your-card` · "What do you get, and when does the card arrive?"

**Answer capsule (55 words)**

> You get a Statement of Attainment for CPCWHS1001 from Upskill Institute, emailed on the day you
> pass, along with a SafeWork NSW statement of training. Upskill Institute lodges your card
> application for you. The plastic card usually arrives within thirty days, and the statement covers
> you for sixty.

**Body**

The gap between finishing the course and holding a card is where most of the worry in this category
sits, so here is the shape of it. You do not lodge anything yourself. Once you pass, Upskill Institute
sends your application to SafeWork NSW, and you are emailed a statement of training you can show on
site straight away.

That statement is valid for sixty days. Your card usually arrives within thirty. The longer number is
deliberate: the statement is designed to outlast the wait, so there is no window where you have
completed the training but cannot legally work. If you pass on a Friday, you can be on site on Monday.

Once the card has been issued you can also carry it digitally in the Service NSW app, which is worth
doing the first time you leave the physical one at home.

**FactGrid**
- Statement of training · valid 60 days
- Plastic card · usually within 30 days
- Issued by · Upskill Institute (RTO 45708)
- Digital card · via the Service NSW app

## S6 · `#how-it-works` · "How does the session run?"

**Answer capsule (54 words)**

> One booked day with a live trainer, about seven hours including breaks, run over video. Sessions
> run Monday to Saturday from 9am to 4pm, with an evening class on Tuesdays from 4pm to 11pm. You
> attend, take part, complete the practical demonstrations and are assessed as you go.

**Body**

Because a trainer runs the session in real time, you book a day rather than starting whenever you
like. Sessions run six days a week, and the Tuesday evening class exists for people who cannot lose a
day's pay to get a ticket they need in order to earn it.

Four things have to be sorted before the session starts, and every one of them stops the day if it is
not. You need a Unique Student Identifier. You need a hundred points of identification. You need to be
physically located in New South Wales while you are being assessed. And you need your own personal
protective equipment in front of you, because part of the assessment is putting it on correctly while
the trainer watches: a hi-vis vest or shirt, a hard hat, ear plugs and safety glasses.

The assessment is conducted in English and you cannot be helped through it by anyone else.

**Stepper**
1. **Book your session.** Choose a weekday, a Saturday, or the Tuesday evening class.
2. **Get your USI and ID ready.** A Unique Student Identifier and a hundred points of identification.
3. **Have your PPE to hand.** Hi-vis, hard hat, ear plugs and safety glasses. You will put them on during the assessment.
4. **Attend the full session.** About seven hours with the trainer, breaks included, from anywhere in New South Wales.
5. **Pass, and get your statement.** Emailed the same day. Upskill Institute lodges your card application for you.

**Note (caution).** You supply your own PPE. The assessment includes fitting and wearing it correctly
on camera, so a session cannot be completed without a hi-vis vest or shirt, a hard hat, ear plugs and
safety glasses.

## S8 · `#faq`

No answer capsule. FAQ repeats what the body already carries; nothing decision-critical lives only
here.

**Can I get a White Card online in NSW?**
Yes. The whole course runs online, with no travel and no classroom. It runs as a booked session with a
live trainer rather than as a self-paced course, because SafeWork NSW requires real-time delivery and
assessment.

**Why can't I do a self-paced White Card in NSW when other states can?**
Fully self-paced online delivery is permitted for Western Australian and Tasmanian residents only.
Everywhere else the training must be face to face, and SafeWork NSW accepts live video as face to
face. Its conditions state that connected delivery must not include self-paced learning or
pre-recorded teaching content.

**How long does the course take?**
About seven hours in one day, including breaks. Sessions run Monday to Saturday from 9am to 4pm, and
there is an evening class on Tuesdays from 4pm to 11pm.

**What do I need before the session starts?**
A Unique Student Identifier, a hundred points of identification, a location in New South Wales for the
duration of the session, and your own hi-vis vest or shirt, hard hat, ear plugs and safety glasses.

**I have a White Card from another state. Do I need a NSW one?**
Usually not. SafeWork NSW accepts construction induction cards issued by other states and territories.
The exception is a card that has gone void, which happens after two consecutive years or more without
construction work.

**How long until my card arrives, and can I work before then?**
Your card usually arrives within thirty days. You can work in the meantime on the statement of
training emailed to you on the day you pass, which is valid for sixty days.

**Does my White Card expire?**
It has no expiry date, but it becomes void if you have not carried out construction work for two
consecutive years or more. After that you need to complete the training again.

**Who issues the Statement of Attainment?**
Upskill Institute, RTO 45708. ABE Education publishes and supports the course and is not a Registered
Training Organisation.
