### Section: Can you do your White Card online in Queensland? (`#online`, marker 03)
- **Claim:** Yes — but it is not self-paced. WHSQ's own conditions define CRTD (live, real-time video
  with a trainer) as a form of face-to-face delivery, and only RTOs separately approved for CRTD may
  run it. Blue Dog Training holds that approval.
- **Reader arrives:** having seen at least one competitor claim QLD is "in person only except for rural
  areas 100km from a classroom" — a real, live, muddled claim found in `02` — or having seen "CRTD"
  without understanding what makes it legitimate.
- **Objection defused:** "Is this actually legal in Queensland, or a workaround?" and "why does one
  provider say rural-only and another say anyone can do it online?"
- **Facts:** CRTD is WHSQ's own definition of a species of face-to-face training (S1/S2 in `01`); the
  2019 >100km rural exception was superseded by the November 2022 CRTD regime and no longer applies —
  CRTD is available to anyone physically located in Queensland, gated only by the RTO's own CRTD
  approval; only 13 of 226 GCIT-approved RTOs hold it; Blue Dog Training has held it since 7 June 2020.
  Location test is **physically located in Queensland**, never "resident" (cond. 37).
- **Distinctive material (Stage 2 gap #1 and #2, the two strongest findings):** this section directly
  corrects a live, findable competitor inaccuracy (the muddled rural/100km claim) with a dated, sourced
  regulator reading, and states the two-step CRTD approval gate no competitor read in `02` mentions.
  This is the page's single strongest E-E-A-T argument.
- **Carrier:** AnswerCapsule + prose (`.measure`) + ResourceLink or a short "check your provider" note
  echoing `#real`'s pattern, applied to the CRTD approval specifically.
- **Fails if:** it states or implies self-paced/pre-recorded content is permitted (cond. 42 bans it
  outright), or attributes the CRTD approval to ABE rather than Blue Dog, or repeats any
  rural/remoteness framing.

### Section: How does the live session work? (`#session`, marker 05)
- **Claim:** One scheduled live online session, at least four and a half hours, with a real trainer and
  a real assessment built in — not a self-paced module and not a quiz at the end.
- **Reader arrives:** wanting to know what "online" actually means here, since it isn't the same as the
  self-paced courses they may have seen advertised for WA or interstate.
- **Objection defused:** "What am I actually signing up for, and how long does it take?"
- **Facts:** minimum 4.5 hours excluding breaks/admin (cond. 40); max 15 students per trainer (cond.
  39); no self-paced or pre-recorded content anywhere in the session (cond. 42); the assessment is the
  Regulator's own mandated assessment, embedded in the live session, not the RTO's own (cond. 36); PPE
  needed **to hand during the session**: eye protection, hearing protection, hard hat, high-visibility
  clothing, **and a copy of the WHS Act 2011 (Qld)** (cond. 38); sessions run at fixed scheduled times
  (Andrey, 3 Aug 2026) — no specific timetable stated, "book the next available session."
- **Distinctive material:** the fifth PPE item (the WHS Act 2011 copy) is in neither competitor's page
  read in `02` — a small, specific, sourced detail.
- **Carrier:** ZSection (image slot — the live session / PPE-ready-at-home moment), Note (variant
  caution) for the PPE list, matching `white-card-wa`'s `#assessment` ZSection pattern but describing a
  materially different format (whole session live, not theory-then-short-assessment).
- **Fails if:** it describes theory as self-paced, states a fixed session length as if it were exactly
  4.5 hours (it's a floor, not the actual scheduled length), or omits the WHS Act copy from the PPE
  list.

### Section: What do you get, and how long does it last? (`#your-card`, marker 07)
- **Claim:** A nationally recognised Statement of Attainment plus a GCIT card (WHSQ's own name for the
  White Card), no expiry, lapses only after two consecutive years out of construction work.
- **Facts:** same national card-validity rules as `white-card-wa` (no expiry, 2-year lapse rule, blue/
  white 2009 history, interstate recognition) — these are not QLD-specific, so reuse them; the one
  QLD-specific fact is that WHSQ calls it the **GCIT card**, worth naming once for search-term match
  ("general construction induction" queries) and reader confidence they have the right document.
- **Carrier:** FactGrid, matching `white-card-wa`.
- **Fails if:** it invents a QLD-specific expiry or refresher rule (none exists).
