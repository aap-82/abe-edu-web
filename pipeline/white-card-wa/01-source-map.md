## C · Discrepancies and cautions found (logged, not silently fixed)

1. **⚠️ The payment endpoint is in Google's index.**
   `/payment?product_id=white-card-wa-enrol&type=course` appears in the site-wide GSC Pages export at
   **102 impressions, position 63.42** over 16 months. A checkout URL should never be indexable: it
   splits crawl signals, exposes a bare form to searchers, and is precisely the class of leak the
   audit workflow's crawl/index control step exists to catch. **Not a blocker for this page**, and not
   fixable from a build session — it needs a `noindex`/robots rule on `/payment`. Routed `[skills]`.

2. **⚠️ WorkSafe WA's own replacement text carries the superseded unit code.** S4 tells a worker whose
   RTO has closed to present "a statement of attainment for course **CPCCWHS1001**" — the superseded
   code. S5, on the same site, correctly uses **CPCWHS1001**, and training.gov.au (S2/S3) shows
   CPCWHS1001 Current and CPCCWHS1001 Superseded to 02 Feb 2027. **The register and training.gov.au
   win: this page uses CPCWHS1001 throughout.** Do not reproduce the regulator's superseded code as
   current even when quoting the replacement process — `system-health` has a standing check for
   exactly this, and it would fail the build.

3. **The register says "WA residents"; the regulator says "located in WA at the time of assessment".**
   Not the same test. A person temporarily in WA at assessment time satisfies S5; a WA resident sitting
   the assessment interstate does not. `online-delivery-policy-by-state.md` line 23 should be tightened
   to the regulator's wording. Routed `[facts]`. **The page uses the regulator's test, not the
   register's paraphrase.**

4. **The register classes WA White Card as commodity self-paced. ABE's WA product is not.** The WA row
   of `competitor-pricing-snapshot.md` explains the $39–$60 WA band as "self-paced online allowed;
   commodity pricing". ABE's WA course carries a **live trainer assessment over Zoom**, which is the
   feature the register itself says drives the $99–$150 QLD/ACT band. ABE at $99 is priced in the
   live-trainer peer set, not the self-paced one. The register's own instruction — "compare ABE within
   its delivery-mode peer set, not across modes" — supports that. Row should carry a note. Routed
   `[facts]`. See `02-gap.md` §5, because it decides how the page is written.

5. **Do not carry TAS facts across.** TAS has a $13.72 fee, an in-person Service Tasmania lodgement
   within 60 days, a WorkSafe Tasmania-issued card and a published minimum age of 14. **None of those
   is true in WA.** WA: no fee, no lodgement trip, RTO-issued card posted to a WA address, no
   published minimum age.

6. **"ABE delivers training" is prohibited.** Blue Dog delivers, assesses, issues the Statement of
   Attainment and issues the card. ABE is the enrolment partner and platform. On an asqa page the RTO
   is also the **developer** — exactly one Person node (Warwick, the reviewer), and a Person titled
   "developer" fails the build.

7. **The data directory moved.** CLAUDE.md and `reference_gsc_export` both say GSC lives in
   `data/GSC/`. It is now `business data/GSC/`, and `data/` holds only `health-log.jsonl`. A run
   following the documented path finds nothing and concludes no export exists. Routed `[skills]`.
