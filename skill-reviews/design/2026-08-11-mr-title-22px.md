# Design review — `.mr-title` to 22px, 2026-08-11

## Verdict

**Green.** A three-times-filed item closed on Andrey's decision, with the objection that held it up
for eleven days measured rather than argued — and found not to materialise where it mattered. All
six consumers verified at two viewports.

## Pre-flight

`node scripts/system-health.mjs` — **0 failing**, 38 warning, 70 ok.

## The decision, and why it was a human's

`ModuleRows`' `.mr-title` was **Archivo 600 18px**, the one size in that component matching no step
in DESIGN.md §3, whose Title is **Archivo 600 22px**. Filed three times (31 Jul, 1 Aug, 10 Aug) and
deliberately not fixed each time, for a stated reason worth quoting because it was a good one:

> ACT renders twelve group titles, and at 22px the rail stops reading as a scannable index and
> starts reading as twelve headings.

That is a hierarchy judgement about a whole section, not a typographic tidy-up, which is why three
design sessions routed it to Andrey instead of deciding it. **Andrey chose 22px on 11 Aug.**

## The objection, measured

The worry was specific and testable: does the rail stop reading as an index? Measured on
`/act-owner-builder-course`, the twelve-title worst case, before and after.

| | 18px before | 22px after |
|---|---|---|
| **Desktop 1280px — titles wrapped** | 0 of 12 | **0 of 12** |
| Desktop — max lines in any title | 1 | **1** |
| Desktop — section height | 1,856px | **1,916px** (+3.2%) |
| **Mobile 375px — titles wrapped** | 8 of 12 | **8 of 12** |
| Mobile — titles at 3 lines | 1 | **3** |
| Mobile — section height | 2,651px | **2,811px** (+6.0%) |
| Page overflow, both viewports | 0px | **0px** |

**The mechanical half of the objection does not happen.** At desktop nothing wraps at either size,
so the one-line-per-row rhythm the 18px note was protecting is intact — twelve titles, twelve
lines, before and after. What changes is weight, not structure.

**The cost is real but small, and it lands on mobile**, where 8 of 12 titles already wrapped at
18px. The same 8 wrap at 22px; two of them go from two lines to three, and the section grows 6%.
No new element wraps that did not wrap before.

Line-height is `1.25` unitless, so it scaled with the size (22.5px → 27.5px) and the internal
rhythm is unchanged.

## Design-register changes

**None.** DESIGN.md §3 already specifies Archivo 600 22px for Title; this makes the code match the
register rather than changing it, so it is conformance and not an exclusive token session (rule 7).
DESIGN.md is untouched.

## The type scale, as a side effect

Against the 15px row body, `.mr-title` now sits at a **1.47 step**, past the 1.25 the scale asks
for. At 18px it was **1.2**, under it. So the change closes a second, quieter gap the original note
had itself recorded.

## The loose end this does NOT close

The 1 Aug filing sharpened the item by noting that **`.faq summary` is also Archivo 600 18px**, so
the undocumented size was shared by two components — and argued that this was a reason to document
an 18px step rather than raise `ModuleRows`.

Andrey's decision was about `.mr-title` specifically. `.faq summary` is **unchanged at 18px**
(`global.css:1000`, verified), and raising a different component with a different job — an accordion
trigger, not a title — was not asked for and is not assumed here.

**So 18px is now held by exactly one rule instead of two.** That makes the original question
narrower and easier, not resolved: either raise `.faq summary` to 22px too, or add 18px to DESIGN.md
as a documented step for accordion triggers. Re-filed below rather than quietly dropped, because the
`.mr-title` decision does not answer it.

## What worked

Measuring the objection instead of re-arguing it. Three sessions had discussed whether 22px would
break the index reading; one headless run across six pages and two viewports answered it in a
minute, and the answer ("nothing wraps at desktop, before or after") is the fact that makes the
change safe rather than a matter of taste.

Also worth recording: routing this to a human was correct and cost exactly one day. The prior
sessions were right not to decide it, and right not to sit on it silently either — it was filed
each time with the trade-off stated, which is why the decision needed no re-explaining.

## What didn't

The comment I first wrote into the component quoted the desktop growth as 1,904px. It is 1,916px.
Caught by re-reading my own note against the measurement before commit rather than after — a wrong
number in a comment outlives the session that wrote it.
