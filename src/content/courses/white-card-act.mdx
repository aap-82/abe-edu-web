<Section bg="bg-alt" id="accepted" marker="02" eyebrow="Accepted and online" h2="Will it be accepted, and can I do it online?">
  <AnswerCapsule>Yes to the first, no to the second. A White Card is nationally recognised the moment it's issued, accepted on every construction site in Australia. But in the ACT, this course is delivered face-to-face in a classroom by AlertForce, not online. That's AlertForce's own arrangement, not a WorkSafe ACT requirement, and other providers may offer it differently.</AnswerCapsule>
  <div class="measure">
    <p>Most sites will not let you start work without a White Card, so the first doubt is settled quickly: once issued, it is accepted everywhere, however the training was delivered. The second doubt is the one worth being direct about. Some providers advertise an online ACT White Card. AlertForce, ABE Education's RTO partner for the ACT, delivers this course face-to-face in a classroom instead.</p>
    <p>Nothing in the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (ACT) requires that. It is simply how AlertForce runs it here, the same as any RTO's own choice of delivery format.</p>
  </div>
  <VerifiedSources date="3 Aug 2026" facts="The WHS Regulation 2011 (ACT) imposes no delivery-mode restriction; face-to-face delivery is AlertForce's own arrangement, not a regulatory requirement" sources={[{ label: 'WorkSafe ACT — White cards (Construction induction)', href: 'https://www.worksafe.act.gov.au/licensing-and-registration/white-cards-construction-induction' }]} />

<Section id="your-card" marker="03" eyebrow="Getting your card" h2="What do you get, and how do you get your card?">
  <AnswerCapsule>Passing the course gives you a Statement of Attainment, not the physical card. To get the card you apply online through an ACT Digital Account within 60 days, and Access Canberra posts it once approved, usually within about two weeks. There's no expiry once it's issued.</AnswerCapsule>
  <div class="measure">
    <p>This is the step most providers do not mention, and the one most likely to catch you out. In the ACT the card is not issued by the course provider. AlertForce gives you the Statement of Attainment; the General Construction Induction Card itself comes from Access Canberra, and only after you apply.</p>
    <p>Your receipt and training certificate let you start work while the application is assessed, which Access Canberra typically completes within about a month. Miss the 60-day window and you will need to arrange a fresh declaration from AlertForce confirming you completed the training, so applying in the first week is the simplest path.</p>
  </div>
  <Stepper steps={frontmatter.howItWorksSteps} maxWidth="560px" />
  <Note variant="caution" label="60-day window">The 60-day window runs from the date on your training certificate. Apply after it and Access Canberra will also require a fresh declaration from AlertForce confirming you completed the training, so applying in the first week is the simplest path.</Note>
