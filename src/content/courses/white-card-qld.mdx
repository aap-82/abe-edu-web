<Section id="online" marker="03" eyebrow="Online in QLD" h2="Can you do your White Card online in Queensland?">
  <AnswerCapsule>Yes, but not as a self-paced course. Workplace Health and Safety Queensland classes Connected Real Time Delivery, a live session with a trainer over video, as a form of face-to-face training in its own right. Only a small number of providers are separately approved to run it.</AnswerCapsule>
  <div class="measure">
    <h3 class="h3">Why some providers say this isn't possible</h3>
    <p>You may have read that Queensland only allows online White Card training in rural areas more than 100 kilometres from a classroom. That rule existed once, but it was replaced in November 2022 when WHSQ introduced Connected Real Time Delivery for everyone in Queensland, regardless of distance from a classroom. Repeating the old rule is the single most common mistake among providers advertising this course.</p>
    <h3 class="h3">The approval most providers don't mention</h3>
    <p>Being approved to deliver the White Card at all is not the same as being approved for CRTD specifically. WHSQ runs a separate two-step process for it: documentation first, then a live test session with a WHSQ inspector. Only 13 of 226 approved providers currently hold it. Upskill Institute holds it [confirm: WHSQ connected real time delivery approval, requested 19 Aug 2026]. Ask any provider whether they hold it specifically, not just whether they are a registered training organisation.</p>
    <h3 class="h3">What the location test actually requires</h3>
    <p>You need to be physically located in Queensland for the session itself. That is not the same as being a Queensland resident: someone working in Queensland temporarily meets it, and a Queensland resident sitting the session from interstate does not.</p>
  </div>
