faqs:
  - q: "Which state's White Card course do I need?"
    a: "The one for the state where you'll be working, not where you live. If you live in Victoria and are working on a site in Tasmania, you need the Tasmanian course."
    open: true
  - q: "How do I actually get my card once I finish the course?"
    a: "Passing the course is the RTO's part, and that's the same everywhere. What happens next differs by state: Western Australia, New South Wales and Queensland issue your card directly once you pass. Tasmania and the ACT are the exceptions - you apply yourself and pay a separate government fee, $13.72 in Tasmania and $47.00 in the ACT. Check your state's own page for the exact steps."
  - q: "Can I do the course online and self-paced?"
    a: "Only in Western Australia and Tasmania. New South Wales and Queensland require a live online session with a trainer, and in the ACT it's face-to-face in a classroom, so budget for a booked time in any of those three rather than working through it whenever suits you."
  - q: "Is my White Card valid in other states once I have it?"
    a: "Yes. The card is nationally recognised the moment it's issued and accepted on construction sites across Australia, regardless of which state's course you completed."
  - q: "Do you offer a White Card course in Victoria, South Australia or the Northern Territory?"
    a: "Not currently. We offer White Card in WA, TAS, NSW, QLD and the ACT. For Victoria, South Australia or the Northern Territory, use a provider licensed in your state."
  - q: "Is ABE Education a registered training organisation?"
    a: "No. Each course is delivered and assessed by a registered training organisation: Blue Dog Training (RTO 31193) in Queensland, Western Australia and Tasmania, Upskill Institute (RTO 45708) in New South Wales, and AlertForce (RTO 91826) in the ACT. ABE Education publishes the course and enrols you; the RTO delivers it and issues your nationally recognised Statement of Attainment."
  - q: "What does the government card fee pay for?"
    a: "It's a separate charge from whichever state or territory authority issues the physical card, on top of the course fee you pay ABE Education. Tasmania charges $13.72 and the ACT charges $47.00 to apply. Western Australia, New South Wales and Queensland charge no separate fee at all, since the training provider issues the card directly."
